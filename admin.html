help: what each tab is for
[1] accounts
Everyone's account: set a password, rename, make admin, fix their handed-in mocks, message, sign out, ban or delete. Add an account or an agent key at the bottom.
[2] activity_log
Who is on the site right now and what they opened. Press trace on a row for one person's history.
[3] sessions
Every signed-in browser. Sign one out, or everyone except you.
[4] hub.json
The home page: its title, subject cards and your own links. Nothing changes on the site until you press commit.
[5] update_notes
The short "what's new" popup for accounts on the list.
[6] site_state
Maintenance page and the full takedown switch.
[7] telemetry
Visitor numbers (only people who accepted cookies).

Keys: press 1 to 7 to switch tab (when you are not typing). Anything that can't be undone asks first. The result of every action shows in the status line under the tabs.
