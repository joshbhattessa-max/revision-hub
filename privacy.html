Privacy Policy

JB Revision · last updated 22 October 2026

This page explains what JB Revision keeps about you, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. In short: your account, your progress and your plan are kept so the site works for you on every device, and while you're signed in the site notes which pages you open so Josh B can see how it's being used; nothing is sold, there are no adverts, and the only optional thing (usage statistics) happens only if you say yes.

Who runs it

JB Revision (jbrevision.co.uk) and its subject sites (ChemQ, BioQ, PhysQ, MathsQ, GeoQ, CSQ, SpanishQ, FrenchQ, LangQ, LitQ, HistoryQ, RPQ, PEQ and DTQ) are run by Josh B, a student, for personal revision. Josh B decides what is kept and why, so Josh B is responsible for your information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Topic sheets you upload as a PDF are read inside your browser: the file itself never leaves your device; only the lines of text you keep are saved with your plan. Choosing questions that match your list also happens entirely in your browser.

Josh B can also see each account's activity (the pages opened and when, which exam papers, the page it's on now and when it was last active) and the mock papers it handed in, to see how the site is used and to help. Your plan is only used to show it back to you.

Cookies and storage on your device

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings (also at the bottom of the home page). Choosing Essential only stops usage statistics straight away and deletes the visitor number.

There are no advertising cookies, no tracking across other websites and no Google Analytics.

Other services the site uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and its data (Cloudflare Pages and Workers KV). Every visit passes through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and protect the site. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to that.
  • Physics & Maths Tutor (pmt.physicsandmathstutor.com). Original papers and mark schemes are PDFs that your browser downloads directly from there, so they see your IP address and browser when you open one, under their own privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) supplies pdf.js, the free reader that draws mark-scheme pages inside the site.
  • Resend (resend.com) sends the emails with codes. It gets your email address and the email itself, under its own privacy policy. Resend is based in the United States, so your address may be handled there.

Nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is sent to any AI service.

Why it's kept (the legal reasons)

  • To give you the service you signed up for: your account (including your email address, which new accounts need so they can reset a forgotten password), sessions, progress and plan.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the site secure (sessions, IP addresses, the security counters), seeing how the site is used so it can be improved and so Josh B can help (your activity), keeping a record of your cookie choice, and answering emails you send.
  • Your consent: usage statistics, which only happen if you press Accept all, and stop when you withdraw it.

How long it's kept

  • Your account, email address, progress, plan and cookie choice: until you delete your account (or change or remove the email address). Each question keeps only its last 8 attempts.
  • Codes emailed to you: 15 minutes, or until they're used.
  • Sign-in sessions, with their IP address, country and device: deleted automatically when they end, after 6 hours, or straight away when you log out.
  • Usage statistics and your activity: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Security counters: 15 minutes (wrong passwords) or 1 hour (new accounts).

Your rights

You can ask to see what's kept about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to object to how it's used. You can do the most important things yourself:

  • Delete your account: the menu under your name on the home page → Delete account. Your account, email address, progress, plan, activity and cookie record are deleted straight away.
  • Change your email address: the same menu → Change email.
  • Withdraw consent to usage statistics: Cookie settings → Essential only.
  • See your progress: it's all on My topics and Your progress.

For anything else, email contact@jbrevision.co.uk (see below). If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

If you're under 13

The site is made for GCSE and IGCSE students. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or carer before you create an account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important will be pointed out on the site. Questions or requests about your information: email Josh B, who runs the site, at contact@jbrevision.co.uk.
